Japanese frontend (Kokoro only)
  Adapted from nerosui/kokoro-js-jp 0.2.0, Apache-2.0.
  https://github.com/nerosui/kokoro-js-jp/tree/8eadd00e25759e5f6d067009dd02943c17048508
  Complete notices and license texts: ./kokoro-japanese.txt.
  Includes misaki (Apache-2.0), cutlet (MIT), Open JTalk/openjtalkjs (BSD),
  Open JTalk dictionary 1.11 (NAIST/UniDic/Open JTalk Modified BSD), and
  Mei HTS voice (Copyright 2009-2013 Nagoya Institute of Technology,
  MMDAgent Project, CC BY 3.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).
  Mei is loaded only because the Open JTalk frontend requires configuration;
  generated speech comes from Kokoro, not the Mei synthesizer.
  Four WASM/wrapper/dictionary/voice assets are copied unchanged from the
  checksum-pinned kokoro-js-jp npm package. No native addon or Python is shipped.
